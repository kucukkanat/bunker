This guide assumes your admin has already [installed Bunker](/bunker/docs/admin/install/) and given you its address, for example `https://bunker.corp.com`.

## 1. Sign in
Open the address. You'll sign in with your normal work Microsoft account and land on **Home**.

## 2. Create a site
Click **New site**, then pick **Build with AI**. Describe what you want in plain words:

> A lunch order form for Friday. People pick a meal and a drink. Show me a summary of all orders with totals.

Bunker builds the app and shows a live preview next to the chat. Keep asking for changes ("make the button green", "add a vegetarian option") until it's right.

## 3. Publish
Click **Publish**. The site gets an address like `https://bunker.corp.com/s/friday-lunch`. Each publish is saved as a [version](/bunker/docs/build/versions/).

## 4. Share
Click **Share** and add people or groups, for example `London Office`. Choose **Can view** or **Can edit**. Until you share it, only you can open the site.

## 5. Send the link
Paste the link into Teams or email. Coworkers open it and they're already signed in.
