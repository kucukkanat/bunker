Assets and full guidelines are in [`/brand`](https://github.com/kucukkanat/bunker/tree/master/brand) in the repository.

![The Bunker mark: a paper arch with an open door on a forest-green tile, standing on a clay ground line](/bunker/favicon.svg)

**Bunker is a safe place to build.** The brand should feel calm, warm and dependable: *safe* to IT, *easy* to everyone else.

## Name

**Bunker** (capital B) in prose; `bunker` in the wordmark, CLI and code. Never "BUNKER", "Bunkr" or "the Bunker".

## Color

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Forest 900 | `#1F3A2E` | Primary: logo, headings, primary buttons |
| Forest 700 | `#2F5543` | Hover, links |
| Moss 500 | `#4F7A63` | Secondary accents, icons |
| Moss 200 | `#C9D8CC` | Borders, subtle fills |
| Paper | `#F7F4EC` | Page background |
| Sand | `#E9E3D3` | Cards, sections |
| Ink | `#1C211E` | Body text |
| Slate | `#5A635D` | Secondary text |
| Clay | `#C2683F` | The one accent. 5% of a layout at most |

Dark mode: background `#121714`, surface `#1A211D`, text `#E8EBE6`, accent `#8FBF9F`, clay `#E08A5F`.

## Type

**Fraunces** for headings, **Inter** for body and UI, **JetBrains Mono** for code. All are open source and bundled with Fontsource; nothing loads from third-party CDNs.

## Voice

- **Plain words.** Write for the person in finance, not the engineer. "Share with your team", not "configure ACLs".
- **Calm confidence.** "Pages can't reach the internet", not "military-grade security".
- **Short.** One idea per sentence.
- **Honest.** Say *Preview* when something isn't finished.

Taglines: *Your own private app cloud.* · *Describe it. Share it. Safely.* · *A safe place to build.*
